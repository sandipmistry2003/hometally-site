HomeTally Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

HomeTally is a household manager for Indian families. It is built so that your family's information stays yours: it is kept locked on your phone, and anything we pass between your family's devices is locked before it leaves the phone, so we can't read it.

This policy explains, in plain words, what HomeTally keeps, where, for how long, and what you can do about it. It covers the HomeTally apps for Android, iPhone and iPad, the HomeTally web app, and the website hometally.in.

The short version

  • Your homes, trackers, bills, documents, photos and notes are stored encrypted on your phone.
  • When your family shares a home, each change is encrypted on the phone before it is sent. Our server only passes on locked copies. It cannot read them.
  • Our server never stores your email address or phone number in readable form, only a keyed scrambled code (a "hash") that lets us recognise your login.
  • No ads. No selling or renting of data. No tracking or analytics inside the app. We never use your phone's location, and we never read your contacts.
  • Voice entry, bill reading and questions are handled on your phone. HomeTally does not send them to any cloud AI.
  • You can export your data, delete a home, or delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

HomeTally is made by Sandip Mistry, an independent developer based in India ("we", "us"). We decide how HomeTally handles personal data.

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy: support@hometally.in

What stays on your phone

Almost everything you put into HomeTally lives only on your devices:

  • Your household data: homes, family members, trackers, bills and payments, warranties, documents, photos, notes and everything else you enter. It is kept in an encrypted database (SQLCipher) whose key is held in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore / Apple Keychain).
  • Photos and files you attach are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they are saved.
  • Your PIN is stored only as a salted hash on the phone. The app lock is always on.
  • Your home's key stays in the phone's secure storage. A laptop or browser only gets it when you link it by scanning a QR code from your unlocked phone, or by entering your recovery key.
  • Your recovery key is shown to you once. We never store it. If you lose your phone and your recovery key, we cannot get your data back.

What our server stores

Our server is there to log you in, pass encrypted changes between your family's devices, and fetch the weather. It keeps:

For your account

  • A keyed hash of your email address (never the email itself).
  • If you add one, a keyed hash of your mobile number (never the number itself).
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple: the account id Google or Apple gives us for HomeTally, and the hash of your verified email.

For each signed-in device

  • The type of device (Android, iPhone/iPad or web), a short label such as "Chrome on macOS" for linked browsers, when it signed in and was last seen, and scrambled sign-in tokens.
  • On Android, a push token from Google so the server can tell your phone "there is something new, please sync". That message contains nothing about your home.

For each shared home

  • Your family's data as encrypted blobs we cannot read, and encrypted copies of the keys your family uses to open them.
  • Information we need to pass changes along, which is not encrypted: random ids for the home, its members, devices and groups, each person's role (for example "admin" or "view-only"), which groups each person can open, the time and order of each change, and the size of attached files. None of this includes names, amounts, notes or any of the content you type.
  • Invitations to join a home: their status and dates, and an encrypted invite message.

For plans and purchases

  • When your free months started and when they end.
  • To give each phone one free period only: on Android, a keyed hash of the phone's Android ID (not linked to your account); on iPhone/iPad, Apple's DeviceCheck service keeps a single yes/no mark for the phone on Apple's side.
  • If you buy Premium: which store, which plan, when it renews or ends, its status, and the purchase reference Google Play or Apple gives us, which we use to check renewals and refunds with the store.

Short-term records

  • One-time login codes are stored only as hashes and expire after 10 minutes.
  • To stop abuse (for example too many login codes), we count requests per network. We store only a scrambled form of your network address, kept for 1 hour.
  • We do not keep the contents of requests in our logs, and our app code does not log email addresses or IP addresses. Our hosting company may keep standard web server logs (which include IP addresses) for a short time for security and operation.

Weather

  • The Today screen shows the weather for the city you chose for your home. The app sends that city's location to our server; our server rounds it to about 11 km and asks MET Norway for the forecast. We never use your phone's GPS location. Forecasts are cached per city, not per person.

Permissions the app asks for

HomeTally asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Camera: to scan the QR code that links a laptop, and to photograph bills and documents.
  • Photos: to pick a bill or document photo you already have.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for voice notes for house help and for speaking an entry. Speech is turned into text on your phone; voice entry is only offered when your phone can do this without the internet.
  • Notifications: for reminders such as bills due and gas refills. They are scheduled on your phone.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: to unlock the app, if you turn it on. Your face or fingerprint never reaches HomeTally.

HomeTally does not ask for your location or your contacts.

When you photograph a bill or receipt, the app reads it on your phone to fill in the form. The plain copy is deleted; if you choose to keep the photo, it is kept encrypted.

Sharing inside your family

When you invite people to a home, they can see what you share with that home, based on the role you give them. You can keep things to yourself with "Only me". The person who created a home can remove members or delete the home for everyone.

How long we keep data

  • On your phone: until you delete it, delete the home, or uninstall the app.
  • Shared home data on our server: while the home exists. When the home is deleted, its data, keys and files are deleted from our server. We keep only the home's random id and the date, so an old phone can't upload it again.
  • Signed-out devices: removed 30 days after sign-out or after their sign-in expires.
  • Login codes: 10 minutes. Network counts: 1 hour.
  • Free-period phone records: kept so each phone gets one free period. They are not linked to your account.
  • Account data: until you delete your account.

Your choices and rights

You can, at any time, from inside the app:

  • See and export your data (Settings → Data), as a readable file or a password-locked file.
  • Correct anything you entered.
  • Leave a home, or delete a home you created.
  • Delete your account. Homes you share with others pass to another member; homes only you use are deleted with their files; then your account and devices are deleted from our server, and the app clears the phone.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you also have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to withdraw consent, to nominate someone to act for you, and to have your complaints addressed. Write to support@hometally.in. Because we can't read your household data, most of it can only be seen, changed or deleted in the app on your own devices.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Delete your account without the app

If you no longer have the app, email support@hometally.in from the email address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We will delete it and tell you when it's done.

Security

Your data is encrypted on your phone and before it is sent; the keys stay on your devices. Connections to our server use HTTPS. We keep what the server stores to a minimum, so that even if it were ever breached, your household data could not be read. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked, use a strong PIN, and keep your recovery key safe.

Children

HomeTally is meant for adults running a household. Parents may record information about their children (for example school or birthdays); it stays encrypted on the family's devices and we cannot read it. If you invite a family member who is under 18, you do so as their parent or guardian.

The web app

The HomeTally web app keeps your linked home in your browser's storage (IndexedDB), locked with a key the browser keeps non-exportable. Display settings are kept in local storage. The web app uses no cookies and no trackers, and can only talk to our own server.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date. If a change matters for how your data is used, we will tell you in the app first.
